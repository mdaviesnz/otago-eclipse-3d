    Otago Eclipse 3D

    Total solar eclipse · 22 July 2028

    These are the best places found to watch the 22 July 2028 total solar eclipse over Otago. The sun will be only 8–10° above the horizon at totality, so each spot is ranked by how far the sun clears the skyline from there — and filtered by whether you can drive on a sealed or gravel road, take a 4WD track, or walk in.

    Every spot was scored against real elevation data across the whole area from which totality is visible, and named viewpoints and sites are cross-checked against public conservation land, reserves, easements, legal road parcels, and trails from Herenga ā Nuku Aotearoa. Open one in the 3D simulator to see the view for yourself, or read how the astronomy & terrain checks work.

    Independent, unvalidated tool — treat results as a guide, not a guarantee. This is an independent project developed with AI assistance and has not yet been independently validated by an astronomer or surveyor (see the technical specification if you can help review it). Elevation data also has limits, and local closures or private land restrictions may apply — check any spot in person before the day, and never look at the partially eclipsed sun without proper eclipse glasses.
