Otago Eclipse 3D

Total solar eclipse · 22 July 2028

These are the best places found to watch the 22 July 2028 total solar eclipse over Otago. The sun will be only 8–10° above the horizon at totality, so each spot is ranked by how far the sun clears the skyline from there — and filtered by whether you can drive, take a 4WD track, or walk in.

Every spot was scored against real elevation data across the whole area from which totality is visible. Open one in the 3D simulator to see the view for yourself.

Please treat results as a guide, not a guarantee. Elevation data has limits, and being near a mapped road is not a right of access — land may be private. Check any spot in person before the day, and never look at the partially eclipsed sun without proper eclipse glasses.
